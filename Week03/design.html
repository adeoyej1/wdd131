Design Principles

Good design principles are essential for creating a positive, engaging user experience.

Design Principles

Typography

Use at most two or three fonts. Choose fonts that are easy to read and visually appealing.

Colors

Use a color scheme that is visually appealing and provides sufficient contrast for readability.

Clarity

Keep the design clean and uncluttered. Use whitespace to guide the user's focus.

Consistency

Maintain a consistent visual language throughout the design.
