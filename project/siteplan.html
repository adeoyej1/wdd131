Startup Business Ideas

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Startup Business Ideas

The site name is Startup Business Ideas. I chose this name because it clearly communicates the main purpose of the website. The website will help aspiring entrepreneurs discover practical business opportunities and learn how to begin a small business.

Proposed domain: startupbusinessideas.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide aspiring entrepreneurs with practical information about different startup business opportunities. The website will provide business ideas, estimated startup costs, required resources, target customers, marketing strategies, and basic steps for starting and growing a business.

The information will be presented in a simple and easy-to-understand format to help visitors compare business opportunities and choose an idea that matches their interests, skills, and available resources.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent the needs of the website's target audience:

  • What business can I start with a small amount of money?
  • How much money do I need to start a particular business?
  • What business idea is suitable for my skills and experience?
  • Who are the potential customers for my business?
  • How can I market my new business and attract customers?

4. Color Scheme

The website will use a professional color scheme that represents growth, trust, opportunity, and success.

Dark Green - #0B5D3B

Dark green will be used for the header, main headings, navigation elements, and important sections. It represents growth, stability, and business development.

Gold - #F4B400

Gold will be used for accents, buttons, borders, highlights, and calls to action. It represents opportunity, achievement, and success.

Light Gray - #F5F7F5

Light gray will be used as the main page background to create a clean and comfortable reading environment.

5. Typography

Montserrat

Montserrat will be used for the site name, headings, navigation, section titles, and other important headings. It provides a modern, professional, and strong appearance.

Roboto

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, lists, and general body content. It is clean and easy to read on both mobile and desktop screens.

6. Target Audience

The primary target audience is aspiring entrepreneurs, students, young professionals, unemployed individuals, and people interested in starting a small business or developing an additional source of income.

7. Planned Website Content

Business Ideas

A collection of practical startup ideas from different business categories.

Startup Costs

Estimated costs and basic resources required to start each business.

Target Customers

Information about the types of customers each business can serve.

Marketing Strategies

Simple marketing methods that can help new businesses attract and retain customers.

Getting Started

Basic steps that aspiring entrepreneurs can follow to turn an idea into an operating business.
